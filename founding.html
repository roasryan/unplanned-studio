    Questions owners ask

    Straight answers.

    Anything else? Ask on your audit call. It's 15 minutes and there's no contract on the call.

    What do the first two partners get?

    Case-study pricing, our lowest. In return we ask for real numbers from the system and, if you're happy, a review and short video. You pay your own ad spend to Google or Meta, and you keep everything we build. Exact numbers come in writing after your free audit.

    What happens after the first two spots are taken?

    Spots 3–5 get founding pricing, locked for 12 months. After spot 5, standard pricing applies. The counter on this page shows how many are left.

    What does it cost?

    A one-time setup, then a share of only the jobs that sign from our ads. No monthly retainer. If we miss the 60-day target, your setup fee comes back. You get exact numbers in writing after your free audit, before you decide anything. Ad spend is separate and goes straight to Google and Meta on your accounts.

    I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

    Usually yes, because most sites only show information. We keep what works (your name, photos, reviews link) and add booking, alerts and the owner app. Your domain stays yours.

    I already use a CRM or invoicing tool.

    Keep it. We plug into what you use (QuickBooks, Square, Jobber, Google Calendar, Slack). We don't replace your invoicing, payments or accounting.

    Do I need to be good with tech?

    No. If you can read a text message you can run it. We set everything up and show you on a short call.

    Is there a contract?

    Yes, a simple one. The first 6 months are set, because ads need about 90 days to learn what a good lead looks like. After that it's month to month with 30 days' notice. Leave and you keep your site, ad accounts and data.

    Who is this not for?

    Companies doing under $150k a year. The system pays off once you have steady jobs and a budget for ads, so we’d rather tell you that now than take your money.

    Can you promise I'll rank #1 on Google?

    No one honest can. We build every page by Google's rules, set up your Google tools and help you get more reviews. Rankings follow over time.

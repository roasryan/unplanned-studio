Privacy policy

Who we are

Unplanned Studio, Houston, Texas ("we"). We build websites, booking and business apps for local businesses. Questions about your information: use the form on our home page and say "privacy".

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms or Meta lead forms: your name, mobile number, email, business type, your current website, what you need, the best way and time to reach you, and whether you agreed to texts. We also record the page you came from and ad campaign tags. If our Meta Pixel is on, Meta may record that you visited and submitted a form.

How we use it

  • To build and send your free demo and contact you about it.
  • To answer your questions and set up a call.
  • To measure which ads work, in total, not to sell your data.

Text messages

We text you only if you check the text box. Texts are about your request: your demo, call times and replies. No marketing blasts. Usually 1–4 messages per request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help.

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your mobile number, SMS opt-in or consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who helps us run this

Google (form storage and email), Meta (ads and lead forms), our text-message provider, and GitHub (website hosting). They process your information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

As long as we're talking about a project, plus any records the law requires. Ask us to delete your information at any time through the form.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP.
  • See, correct or delete your information: contact us through the form.
  • Our forms are for business owners 18 and older.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top.

Where jobs leak out

Most companies don't need more leads first. They need to stop losing the ones they pay for.

  • Calls you can't answerYou're on a roof, under a sink or with a client. They call the next name on Google.
  • Ad leads nobody answers fastYou pay $100–$500 for a lead, it sits in an inbox for hours, and they book the company that called first.
  • A site that only "informs"No way to book, no price range, no text button. People leave to find one that does.
  • Jobs scattered everywhereTexts, notebooks, three apps. Things slip, customers ask "any update?", reviews never get asked for.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup plus monthly management, priced for your trade, area and how many channels we run. You get exact numbers in writing after your free audit, before you decide anything. Ad spend is separate and goes straight to Google and Meta on your accounts. Most of our partners already spend 7–10% of revenue on marketing; this replaces the parts that weren't working.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Usually yes, because most sites only show information. We keep what works (your name, photos, reviews link) and add booking, alerts and the owner app. Your domain stays yours.

I already use a CRM or invoicing tool.

Keep it. We plug into what you use (QuickBooks, Square, Jobber, Google Calendar, Slack). We don't replace your invoicing, payments or accounting.

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. If you can read a text message you can run it. We set everything up and show you on a short call.

Is there a contract?

12 months for management, because ads need about 90 days to learn what a good lead looks like. After that it's month to month. Leave and we hand over your site, ad accounts and data.

Who is this not for?

Companies under about $500k a year, or anyone who can't put $2,000 a month into ads yet. We'd rather tell you that now than take your money.

Can you promise I'll rank #1 on Google?

No one honest can. We build every page by Google's rules, set up your Google tools and help you get more reviews. Rankings follow over time.

Your growth plan for hvac in Houston.

Made for HVAC companies doing under $150k a year. Six steps, in order. Most cost nothing but a few hours. Do one a week.

  1. Fix your Google Business Profile

    Set your primary category to HVAC contractor or Air conditioning contractor. List each service on its own: AC repair, AC replacement, heating repair, maintenance tune-ups, duct cleaning, thermostats. Add your real hours, service area and 10+ photos of your own jobs. Google says complete, accurate profiles are more likely to show up in local search.

  2. Ask every happy customer for a review

    Ask right after the air is blowing cold again and the house starts to cool. That's when the homeowner is most relieved. Text the link before you back out of the driveway. Reply to every review, good or bad. Google counts reviews and how you respond as part of how well-known you are.

  3. Answer fast, or text back fast

    When an AC dies on a 98-degree afternoon, people call down the list until someone picks up. A missed call is usually a lost job. If you can't answer, text back within a minute with a time you can be there. One well-known Harvard Business Review study found companies that responded within an hour were about 7 times more likely to qualify the lead than ones that waited longer.

  4. Follow up on every quote

    For replacement quotes, send the written options the same day, then check in on day 2 and day 7. Most people are comparing two or three quotes and pick the company that stayed in touch. When two quotes are close, the job often goes to the company that stayed in touch.

  5. Sell a maintenance plan

    Two tune-ups a year for a flat yearly price. Members call you first when something breaks, and the spring and fall visits fill your slow weeks. Start by offering it on every repair call.

  6. Track where every job comes from

    Keep one sheet with five columns: date, name, where they found you, booked (yes/no), job value. Fill it in every time the phone rings or a form comes in. After 30 days you'll know which source pays and which one only costs you money.

Your next 30 days

  • Week 1: Google profile complete, every service listed, 10+ real photos.
  • Week 2: Review text ready to send. Ask every customer this week.
  • Week 3: Missed-call text set up on your phone. Every quote followed up.
  • Week 4: Start the tracking sheet and do step 5.

Timing tip: Book spring tune-ups in February and March, before the first heat wave fills your schedule.

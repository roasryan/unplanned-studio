Home service work in The Woodlands

The Woodlands is a township north of Houston, mostly in Montgomery County, known for its heavy tree cover and strict residential design review. Exterior projects often need approval before work starts, so fence, roofing and remodeling jobs take longer to close and need more follow-up.
